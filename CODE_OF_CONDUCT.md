# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Participation in this project should be respectful, technically constructive,
and welcoming. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss ideas and mistakes without personal attacks;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints;
- accept correction and take responsibility for harmful behavior; and
- keep private information, credentials, and vulnerability details private.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing this project. Technical
disagreement is welcome; abusive conduct is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer at
[`kid@ml1.net`](mailto:kid@ml1.net). Include links or other context needed to
understand the incident. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
personal information.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may
remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the community. Retaliation against someone
who makes a good-faith report is itself a violation.

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
